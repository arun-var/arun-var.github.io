I built this site to respect your privacy completely. There's nothing to opt out of, because there's nothing collecting your data in the first place.

## What this site does *not* do

- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies of any kind — not for analytics, not for advertising, not for "preferences."
- **No analytics.** There's no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no pixels, and no third-party analytics of any kind. I don't count you.
- **No ads and no ad networks.** Nothing on the page is loading trackers to profile you.
- **No fingerprinting.** No scripts trying to identify your device or browser.

## What does happen

- The site is served as static HTML. Like any web host, the server may keep basic, short-lived access logs (such as the requesting IP address) for security and reliability. These aren't used to profile or identify you, and aren't shared or sold.
- A couple of resources may load from reputable third parties (for example, jQuery from a public CDN, and web fonts). These can see a request came in, but the site sends no identifying information and sets no cookies through them.
- If you use the contact form, the details you type are sent to a form-handling service solely so I can read and reply to your message.

## Want to verify it yourself?

Open your browser's developer tools on any page here:

- **Application → Cookies**: you'll see none set by this site.
- **Network** tab: no requests to analytics or ad domains.

That's the whole policy. If anything ever changes, this page will say so.
